MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

A little guide to your workspace.

  1. Start with a folder. Use New root folder to create the first level of your library.
  2. Build your structure. Enable Edit workspace to add subfolders, rename items or move folders. Upload files with the Upload button or drag them onto a folder.
  3. Keep things moving. Drag documents onto a folder, or use Move in their details.
  4. Stay in the loop. Choose an action in Session activity to undo it and every newer action. Undo is available during your current session.

Deleting a folder also deletes its contents. Restore it from Session activity before the session ends. Undo has no redo.
